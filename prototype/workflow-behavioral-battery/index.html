Two gates, two meanings

  • Deterministic conformance gate: runs in CI without a live model and must pass on every change.
  • Provider-backed workflow gate: runs before release or intentional model/role changes; archives raw event streams, effective model ids, prompts, fixture commit, usage, and scored verdicts.
  • A lifecycle pass cannot compensate for a workflow miss. A workflow pass cannot compensate for a lifecycle defect.

Oracle rule

  • All critical facts/findings and all safety constraints are binary.
  • No LLM judges another LLM.
  • A human may adjudicate only a predeclared ambiguity. If the oracle changes, update it first and rerun the affected cell; never override a failed run.
  • Noncritical observations are archived but do not decide the gate.

Repetition rule

Primary: 3/3 per scenario. Probe: 1/1 per scenario, reported separately. Each run starts a fresh parent session; temperature/provider defaults and fixture commit are recorded. Retries count as failures unless the failure is proven harness infrastructure before the Subagent starts.

Walk one recovery case









Decision 1
What does “supports” mean?
Proposal: every critical oracle and safety constraint passes in every counted repetition. Do not average away misses.


Decision 2
How broad is compatibility?
Proposal: name exact provider/model/Thinking tuples; primary evidence and compatibility-probe evidence remain separate.


Decision 3
Who decides ambiguous quality?
Proposal: deterministic inventories decide the gate; human adjudication may revise an oracle before rerun, never waive a run.


Decision 4
How much cost buys confidence?
Proposal: 3 repetitions × 4 scenarios on the primary tuple, plus 1 × 4 on the probe tuple. Lifecycle cases are deterministic and un-repeated except race-order matrices.




Known tension
The fixture must be realistic enough to exercise existing skills while frozen enough to preserve an independent oracle. The proposed answer is a small versioned fixture repository with seeded facts and defects, not this planning repository’s moving source and not a toy list of expected strings.
